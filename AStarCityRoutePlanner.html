Problem definition

Problem: Find a minimum-cost route between two locations in a city represented as a weighted graph.

AI technique: A* heuristic search. Locations are states; roads connect states; road costs form the path cost.

Heuristic: Straight-line distance to the destination. Values are shown to two decimal places in scaled distance units. The city map is illustrative, not live geographic data.

f(n) = g(n) + h(n)
g(n) — Actual costCost travelled from the source to node n.
h(n) — HeuristicEstimated remaining cost from node n to the goal.
f(n) — EvaluationEstimated total route cost through node n. A* selects the available node with the lowest f-score.
